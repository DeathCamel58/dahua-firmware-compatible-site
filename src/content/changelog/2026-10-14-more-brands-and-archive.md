---
title: 18 more brands, and every file saved for good
date: 2026-10-14
category: data
---

Another big update: about 750 more firmware files, for nearly 8,700 in total, now from 45 brands and sources.

- **More places to find firmware.** New sources include Global DS (Hungary), TVC (Mexico), SNR, Lupus Electronics, Sveistrup, Santronic, BITS, Winco, Security Point (Italy), Dahua India, the Eltrox family of brands (Kenik, EasyCam, Notis, Zeus, Irbis and Konig), and community collections shared on IPCamTalk and ip-phone-forum.de. Dahua's own servers also turned up older builds that are no longer on its download pages.
- **Saved for good.** Almost every file now has a permanent copy on the Internet Archive, so it stays downloadable even after a vendor removes it.
- **Better matching.** Nearly 7,000 files are now matched to the hardware they run on, about 1,000 more than before, and around 1,000 more retail model names are listed.
- **Clearer labels.** Firmware that can't be read is now explained instead of marked as failed: encrypted firmware (common for newer recorders) is labelled as such, and microcontroller firmware, which updates a small part like a PTZ motor controller rather than the device itself, is marked separately.

As always, new files appear on [Newly added](/new/), and flashing firmware is at your own risk.
