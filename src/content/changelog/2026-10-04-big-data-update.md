---
title: Nearly 8,000 firmware files from 27 brands
date: 2026-10-04
category: data
---

The firmware list has grown from about 1,400 files to almost 8,000, collected from 27 brands and distributors.

- **Many more brands.** Dahua's own download sites, plus EmpireTech, ENS Security, Inaxsys, VIP Vision, Rhino, Montavue, RVI, Speco, Optiview, IC Realtime, Intelbras and others, and distributor download servers in Europe and Ukraine.
- **Rescued firmware.** About 800 files that their vendors no longer offer were recovered from old copies saved by the Wayback Machine.
- **Better matching.** Nearly 6,000 firmware files are now matched to over 6,000 devices, so it's easier to find firmware for the same camera sold under another brand's name.
- **Know your chip.** Many firmware pages now show the chip the firmware is built for, and you can [browse firmware by chip](/soc/).
- **Safer flashing.** Firmware pages warn when installing a file may stop you going back to older firmware, flag incomplete downloads that can't be installed, and point out Hikvision firmware that some vendors list alongside Dahua's.

Some firmware still can't be analysed, mostly encrypted recorder images, and a few more downloads are on the way. New files appear on [Newly added](/new/) as they arrive.
