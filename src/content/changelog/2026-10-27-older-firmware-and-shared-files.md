---
title: Older firmware, and files shared by the community
date: 2026-10-27
category: data
---

About 880 more firmware files have been added, for over 9,500 in total, now from 50 brands and sources.

- **Older firmware.** Dahua's download files from 2016 to 2018, which are still online but no longer linked from its site, and RVI's old file server, with upgrade files for each model from 2012 to 2018.
- **Files shared by the community.** Firmware posted on forums or shared through Yandex Disk, Google Drive, OneDrive, MEGA and Dropbox. Each file's page says where it was shared.
- **New brands and sources.** Sapsan, Vidimost, Hybrone, Goliath and Imou, plus older Dahua, Amcrest and Lorex files listed on Softpedia. ASM and dahuawiki also turned up more files.
- **Rescued before it vanished.** A Vietnamese KBVision and Dahua download server went offline this month. Its files were recovered from Wayback Machine copies.
- **Better matching.** About 800 more files are matched to the hardware they run on, nearly 7,800 in all, and almost 900 more retail model names are listed.
- **Cut-off copies point to the full file.** Some files turned out to be just the start of another file. Their pages now send you to the complete file instead of offering the broken copy.

As always, new files appear on [Newly added](/new/), and flashing firmware is at your own risk.
