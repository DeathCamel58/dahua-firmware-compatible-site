---
title: Easier browsing and faster pages
date: 2026-10-04
category: site
---

With so much more firmware, the site has been reorganised so it stays quick to find your way around:

- **[Retail models](/model/) by brand.** Pick the brand that sold your device to see its models. Brands with thousands of models, like Dahua, are split into series such as IPC-HFW or NVR.
- **Shorter pages.** Long lists of firmware, on device, chip, brand and year pages, are now split into pages of 100, newest first. Brand pages show their newest firmware and most common models, with links to everything else.
- **More detail where it helps.** Firmware pages can now show the chip, Linux kernel and flash layout, and list the earlier addresses a file was published at, which helps when hunting for copies.
- **Faster loading.** Brand logos are smaller and load only when needed.
- **Old links still work.** Pages that were merged or retired now forward to the closest current page.
